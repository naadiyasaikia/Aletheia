Welcome to Tutorial

=====
  HOW TO PLAY  (1/4)

  You are a magical girl standing her ground.
  Shadow monsters appear on the right side of the screen
  and attack you over time.

  Survive and defeat 15 enemies to win the game.

  If your HP reaches 0, the round is over.

  Skills are not instant: each one plays a short casting
  animation first, and the damage lands when it finishes.
=====
  CONTROLS  (2/4)

  Every girl has 4 skills, one for each number key:

     [1]  Basic attack    fast, use it again and again
     [2]  Strong attack   slower, hits harder
     [3]  Defense / heal  survive the tough moments
     [4]  Ultimate        huge power, needs time to charge

  While a skill is casting, you cannot use another one.
  Enemies keep attacking, so time your casts!
=====
  READING THE SCREEN  (3/4)

  Bottom left shows your skills:

     [1] Petal Burst  ██████████ READY
     [2] Thorn Shower ███░░░░░░░ 1.8s

  A full bar means the skill is ready.
  The bar fills while the cooldown counts down.

  READY x2 means the skill has limited uses left.
  EMPTY means no uses remain this round.

  The ultimate starts empty: wait for it to charge.
=====
  TIPS  (4/4)
  - Customize your girl before the fight. Each one plays
    differently: press [c] on the title screen.
  - Healing has limited uses, save it for emergencies.
  - Mist Drift blocks the next hit. Lunar Veil cuts damage
    by 70% for a short time. Tempest Waltz makes you
    invulnerable while it plays.
  - Area skills shine when enemies pile up.
  - Fast Bats hurt less per hit; Golems hit very hard but slowly.

  Good luck, magical girl!
